Learn the move:
Flowers.

The ‘Flowers’ is a dance move inspired by the energetic Bhangra folk dance and the beautiful traditions of Indian ceremonies. This move symbolises the blooming of a flower or the act of throwing flower petals, a significant gesture in Indian weddings and other important ceremonies. The ‘Flowers’ move, with its roots in traditional Indian dance and its adaptability to modern rhythms, brings a unique blend of culture and creativity to any dance routine. Remember, each dancer brings their own interpretation to a move, so feel free to express the ‘Flowers’ in your own style. Happy dancing!

Try the footwork first. Add the hands once the rhythm feels natural.
