01 · Premiered 7 November 2026 · Shoonya Theatre, Ghent

Sangam

A village without water, and a child who belongs to no one. Seventy minutes of Indian folk, Bollywood and semi-classical dance.

It premiered at Shoonya Theatre, Ghent, on 7 November 2026, and both performances, on 7 and 8 November, sold out.

Sangam credits · Concept, direction & choreography: Swapnil Dagliya, in collaboration with the ABC dancers · Production: Wim Boussery & Swapnil Dagliya · Dancers: Swapnil Dagliya, Narcisse Merlier, Sara Van Holm, Svetlana Bubnova, Chiara Bisinelli, Kaushika Kumar, Laurien De Ridder, Khushboo Agarwal, Shreya Vaidya, Roshni Tela, Haike Bourgeois & Srimahavalli Thiyagarajan · Lighting: Leander Leeman · Costumes: ABC a bollywood company · An ABC a bollywood company production at Shoonya Dance Centre

02 · Premiered 2024

The Four Loves

The Four Loves explores devotion (bhakti), familial affection (vatsalya), romance (shringar) and universal connection (prema) through Ghoomar, Chari, Tera Taali, Garba, Bhangra, Bollywood and Kathak-shaped semi-classical work.

The Four Loves credits · Choreography: Swapnil Dagliya · Production: Wim Boussery & Swapnil Dagliya · Dancers: Chiara Bisinelli, Kaushika Kumar, Khushboo Agarwal, Laurien De Ridder, Narcisse Merlier, Roshni Tela, Sara Van Holm, Shreya Vaidya, Svetlana Bubnova, Siddhy Shetty & Swapnil Dagliya · Guest performer: Pranali Sanghmitra Bhagwat · Lights: Johan Van Compernolle · Sound: Wim Boussery · Dramaturgy: Rita Hendricks · Trailer: shot by Marc Antoine, edited by Rajat Senjaliya · Supported by Shoonya Dance Centre
