## Design Context

### Users
1. **Programmers and bookers** — theatres, festivals, city squares, company
   evenings, weddings. The commercial core. Job: *is this company right for my
   stage, on my date, within my budget, and who do I ask?* They skim, they need
   scale and range fast, and they leave if the booking route is a maze.
2. **Prospective dancers in Ghent** — the free course, the trial week, the
   starter series, the weekly timetable. Job: *where do I start?*
3. **Festival audiences and artists** — GIDF Edition Five, 7–9 May 2027.

ABC is a **company**, not a school: the studio brand is Shoonya, and weekly
classes and festival enquiries legitimately hand off there.

### Brand personality
**Live · plural · warm.** The site's own line: *"Not one dance. Not one stage.
Not one way to move."* ABC is thirteen dancers who rehearse every week in Ghent —
a real ensemble, not a name on a poster. Founded **2017**.

- **ABC leads in English** (D-110, since 2026-09-02). Shoonya stays Dutch-first.
- Never present a **solo as the ensemble** — when the copy says "company", the
  photograph must show the company (a documented past error on this build).

### Aesthetic direction
- **Cobalt · marigold · night · bone**, plus red *punctuation only*.
  - ABC's vibrant is **marigold `#FBC70F`**.
  - **Bindu red `#E5231B` is punctuation, never a plane (D-066 + D-123):**
    separator dots up to ~8px are fine; any red fill, panel, stamp, badge or
    red type is not.
  - Foundation light ground is **bone `#F4F1EB`** (D-071), never pure white.
- **GIDF is programme-coded (D-102)** — no single festival vibrant: purple hub ·
  pink opening · saffron workshops · yellow gala. **GIDF has no standalone mark
  (D-073)**: wordmark only.
- The build is a **cinematic scroll**: overture film, pinned manifesto, acts with
  their own worlds, postcards, curtain call. Imagery performs; practical
  information stays plain. That split is the governing idea.

### Anti-references
- Squarespace-template company site: hero slider, three service cards,
  testimonial band, "Get in touch" footer.
- Bollywood-as-kitsch. The forms have real names and real regions.
- **Bollywood Bandits imagery is banned on ABC surfaces** — a separate Swiss
  group. (GIDF festival records are the deliberate exception, D-104.)

### Design principles
1. **Every fact appears once per surface (D-024).** The recurring failure on this
   build is the same date or price printed three and four times on one page.
2. **Imagery must be provenanced.** Right event, right festival, company vs solo,
   credit from EXIF or left blank — never guessed (D-050, D-051).
3. **Motion serves the narrative (D-037).** Reduced motion must leave every scene
   complete and static.
4. **No email address in markup (D-074).** Booking and coaching run through the
   on-page forms; classes and festival hand off to Shoonya.
5. **The booking route is the primary action.** If a scene is beautiful and the
   way to book is not obvious from it, the scene is wrong.
